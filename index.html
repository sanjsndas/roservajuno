About Us

Roservajuno is a home flower and plant care company created for people who want to enjoy beautiful, healthy greenery without worrying about complicated maintenance. We believe that plants make a home feel warmer, calmer, and more alive, but every plant has its own needs. Some require carefully controlled watering, others need more light, seasonal feeding, pruning, repotting, or protection from pests. Our job is to understand those needs and provide thoughtful, reliable care that helps your plants flourish. Whether you have a few favorite houseplants on a windowsill or an extensive indoor collection, Roservajuno gives every plant the individual attention it deserves.

Our work goes beyond simply watering flowers. We look at the complete environment in which a plant lives: its location, access to natural light, temperature, humidity, soil condition, pot size, watering schedule, and overall appearance. These details help us understand why a plant is thriving—or why it may be struggling. Yellow leaves, dry tips, slow growth, drooping stems, or recurring pests can all be signs that something needs to change. Roservajuno helps identify these problems early and recommends practical solutions designed for the conditions in your home. Our goal is not only to improve how your plants look today, but also to create the right conditions for healthy growth in the months and years ahead.

Our Approach

At Roservajuno , we approach plant care with patience, observation, and respect for natural growth. We do not believe in one universal routine for every houseplant. A tropical plant in a bright living room may need completely different care from a succulent on a bedroom windowsill or a delicate flowering plant in a shaded corner. That is why we begin by assessing each plant and its surroundings before deciding what should be done. We consider watering frequency, light exposure, drainage, soil structure, humidity, seasonal changes, and the plant’s current condition. From there, we build a simple and practical care routine that supports healthier growth without unnecessary treatments or constant intervention.

We also want professional plant care to feel easy and comfortable for the people who invite us into their homes. Our recommendations are clear, realistic, and suited to everyday life. If moving a plant closer to a window can improve its condition, we will explain why. If a different pot, soil mixture, or watering schedule would help, we will recommend an appropriate change rather than complicating the process. We can also help homeowners better understand their plants, recognize common warning signs, and avoid frequent mistakes such as overwatering, poor drainage, unsuitable placement, or excessive fertilizing. With Roservajuno , professional care becomes a partnership between our knowledge, your home environment, and the natural needs of your plants.
